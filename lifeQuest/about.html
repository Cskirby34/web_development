Features of LifeQuest

What is our goal?

LifeQuest is a modular website that shows you your progress on your personal skills every day.

Features

Keep Track of your Money
Use LifeQuest to keep track of how much money you spent. Additionally you can use it to form a budget plan and keep track of how much you have left.

Healthy Habit Tracker
LifeQuest allows users to log info on what they ate, how much water they drank, how much they slept and how much they are exercising. This allows users to check how they are doing and see if any adjustments need to be made for a healthier lifestyle.

Check How Much you have been Productive
